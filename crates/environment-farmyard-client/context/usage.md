# Using the Farmyard's Client Half

`register(sceneries)` registers the farmyard with a client's `tomori_render::scenery::Sceneries`: its scenery, at `ID` and `VERSION`, the identity in `tomori-environment-farmyard`; its teleport profile (`locomotion()`), the yard's earth and the trail round the outside of the fence, with a pair of pads between the yard and the trail's lookout; its client side, which shows the chickens; its ambience (`ambience::ambience()`); and the chicken's licence notice, `LICENSE`, under `LICENSE_TITLE`, and its sounds', `ambience::LICENSE`, under `ambience::LICENSE_TITLE`, which `tomori --licenses` shows. `register_with(sceneries, coop)` does so with another coop, such as one modelled in Blender (see `tomori-environment-farmyard`'s `context/coop.md`).

- `scenery::Farmyard` is the `Scenery`: the evening sky, the yard, the coop and, through `Scenery::animate`, the chickens, drawn with `tomori-assets`' renderer and lit by `scenery::lighting()`; and the backdrop, its foothills and bush drawn the same way, its water with `water.wgsl` and its ranges at the sky's depth with `sky.wgsl` (see `tomori-environment-farmyard`'s `context/backdrop.md`); the river's stones and the trail, models too; the ferns, every kind's mesh instanced where `tomori-environment-farmyard`'s `ferns::all` says, near or far by their distance from the eyes, swayed in the breeze and silver beneath by `fern.wgsl` (see `context/ferns.md` there); and the trail's stations' plaques, panels of text drawn from the renderer's glyph atlas (see `context/trail.md` there). Its water moves with the time since it was made; `Farmyard::with_clock` holds it at a moment, `scenery::Clock::Held`, for pictures that must be the same each time.
- `ambience` declares the farmyard's ambience, by slot, `ambience::SLOTS`: wind and bush everywhere, the river and the waterfall where the backdrop has them, and birds from its bush. Its sounds are `assets/sounds/SLOT.ogg`, which the build finds, by digest (`ambience::files()`), and embeds but on Android; a slot without one is silent. `assets/sounds/readme.md` says what each should be.
- `client::FarmyardClient` is the `ClientSide` of one space: it reads the flock's state and events from the farmyard's server side, keeps the farmyard's clock from them, drives a `ChickenController` for each chicken, picks the chicken the ray meets, asks the server side to notice it and to stop, and returns the clucks to play and the chickens to draw (`client::Drawn`).

A headset build that registers it should carry `tomori-environment-farmyard`'s `assets/models` in its APK as `farmyard/`, and this crate's `assets/sounds` as `farmyard/sounds/`, as Tomori's own does in `android/assets`, licence notices and all; see `tomori-client-quest`'s `context/android.md`. The chicken is by Hinoki Maple Williams, under CC BY 4.0, which asks that whoever shares it credits her and links the licence, as that notice does.

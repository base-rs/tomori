# The Farmyard's Sounds

The sounds in this folder each keep the licence their author gave them, as listed here, beside its author, where it came from and what was changed. Wherever the farmyard's sounds go, this notice goes with them: the farmyard's client crate adds it to a client's licences, which `tomori --licenses` shows, and the APK carries it as `assets/farmyard/sounds/LICENSE.md`.

The farmyard bundles no sounds yet. Each one added is listed below, as:

- `SLOT.ogg`: what it is, by its author, from where it was found (a link), under its licence (a link), and what was changed, such as trimmed, made to loop, mixed down to mono, resampled or made quieter.

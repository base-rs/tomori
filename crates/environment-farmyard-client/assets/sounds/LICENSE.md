# The Farmyard's Sounds

The sounds in this folder each keep the licence their author gave them, as listed here, beside its author, where it came from and what was changed. Wherever the farmyard's sounds go, this notice goes with them: the farmyard's client crate adds it to a client's licences, which `tomori --licenses` shows, and the APK carries it as `assets/farmyard/sounds/LICENSE.md`.

Every sound below was converted to 48 kHz Ogg Vorbis (quality 4) and its level set to −23 LUFS integrated with its true peak at or below −3 dBTP; each entry lists what else was changed.

## Beds and water

- `wind.ogg`: wind in tall pines in a quiet forest, by bruno.auzet, from "silent windy pine forest.wav" on Freesound (https://freesound.org/people/bruno.auzet/sounds/670307/), recorded in the forêt de Rohanne, France, under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Changed: 58 s taken from 1:44 of Freesound's preview, high-passed at 40 Hz and low-passed at 6 kHz (which also removes faint distant birds), made into a 54 s stereo loop by crossfading its last 4 s into its start, and begun 23.3 s into that loop, at its quietest, so that its join is not heard.
- `bush.ogg`: a night ambience of crickets and a gentle breeze in trees near a campground in rural New Zealand, by robotjay, from "Ambience Night Rural Campground New Zealand" on Freesound (https://freesound.org/people/robotjay/sounds/518555/), under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Changed: 56 s taken from 0:02 of Freesound's preview, high-passed at 60 Hz, and made into a 52 s stereo loop by crossfading its last 4 s into its start.
- `river.ogg`: a stream's steady flow at night in Āwhitu Regional Park, New Zealand, by robotjay, from "Stream Flow Night Ambience" on Freesound (https://freesound.org/people/robotjay/sounds/518556/), under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Changed: 49 s taken from 0:05 of Freesound's preview, mixed down to mono, high-passed at 60 Hz, made into a 45 s loop by crossfading its last 4 s into its start, and its loudest splashes limited by up to 3.8 dB.
- `waterfall.ogg`: a small waterfall and a creek running over rocks in Whakanewha Regional Park, Waiheke Island, New Zealand, by brentsimpson, from "Small waterfall, creek running over rocks - Waiheke Island, Aotearoa / New Zealand" on Freesound (https://freesound.org/people/brentsimpson/sounds/727804/), under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Changed: 49 s taken from 0:10 of Freesound's preview, mixed down to mono, high-passed at 40 Hz, made into a 45 s loop by crossfading its last 4 s into its start, and its loudest splashes limited by up to 5.5 dB.

## Birds

The tūī, bellbird and kererū are the Department of Conservation's recordings: Source: Department of Conservation (NZ), https://www.doc.govt.nz/nature/native-animals/birds/bird-songs-and-calls/. They are Crown copyright, licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), as DOC's copyright page says (https://www.doc.govt.nz/footer-links/copyright/).

- `tui-1.ogg`, `tui-2.ogg`, `tui-3.ogg`: three phrases of a male tūī's song, from DOC's "Tūī territorial calls" (https://www.doc.govt.nz/globalassets/documents/conservation/native-animals/birds/bird-song/tui-song-50.mp3), at 0:56.1 to 1:01.66, 1:04.4 to 1:09.9 and 1:10.4 to 1:14.1. Changed: trimmed to each phrase, mixed down to mono, high-passed at 500 Hz, low-passed at 7 kHz, a faint 7.3 kHz tone notched out, the steady background lowered by spectral noise reduction, and faded over 5 ms in and 40 to 80 ms out.
- `bellbird-1.ogg`, `bellbird-2.ogg`, `bellbird-3.ogg`: three phrases of an adult male bellbird's (korimako's) song, from DOC's "Bellbird/korimako adult male" (https://www.doc.govt.nz/globalassets/documents/conservation/native-animals/birds/bird-song/bellbird-56.mp3), at 0:13.7 to 0:15.6, 0:31.3 to 0:32.9 and 0:05.7 to 0:08.5. Changed: trimmed to each phrase, mixed down to mono, high-passed at 900 Hz, low-passed at 9 kHz, the background lowered by spectral noise reduction, and faded over 5 ms in and 30 ms out.
- `kereru-1.ogg`, `kereru-2.ogg`: a kererū's wingbeats as it flies, from DOC's "New Zealand pigeon/kererū/kūkū/kūkupa" (https://www.doc.govt.nz/globalassets/documents/conservation/native-animals/birds/bird-song/nz-pigeon-song.mp3), at 0:10.7 to 0:12.9 and 0:20.3 to 0:22.3. Changed: trimmed to each flight, mixed down to mono, high-passed at 120 Hz, low-passed at 5 kHz (removing most of the cicadas behind them), the remaining background lowered by spectral noise reduction, `kereru-2`'s peaks limited by up to 0.6 dB, and faded over 5 ms in and 40 to 60 ms out.
- `ruru-1.ogg`, `ruru-2.ogg`: a ruru's (morepork's) "more-pork" call, two of the three in the recording, by eklemp, from iNaturalist observation 413846348 (https://www.inaturalist.nz/observations/413846348), recorded near Motueka, Tasman, New Zealand, credited as iNaturalist asks: (c) eklemp, some rights reserved (CC BY); under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changed: trimmed to the calls at 0:05.4 and 0:10.0, high-passed at 350 Hz, low-passed at 2 kHz (removing a band of insects at about 2.5 kHz), the background lowered by spectral noise reduction, and faded over 5 ms in and 60 ms out.

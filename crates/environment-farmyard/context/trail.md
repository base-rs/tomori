# The Farmyard's Trail

A walking trail loops round the outside of the fence: out through the gate, along the front to the right, down the yard's right side along the river's bank, round behind the coop to a lookout over the waterfall's pool, with a log seat, along the edge of the bush on the left, and back to the gate, about 61 m. Five exercise stations stand beside it, each a post with a plaque that says what to do there, standing, with a headset on. It is all in `trail.rs`, laid out from `trail::WAYPOINTS`.

## Where it goes

`trail::centreline()` is the loop, smoothed through `WAYPOINTS`, `STEPS` points to each, the first at the gate; `trail::frame(index)` gives a point of it, the way along it and the way out of the loop. Its track is `WIDTH`, 1.2 m, from crest to crest, all of it walkable. It keeps:

- **within `REACH`**, 11.5 m of the backdrop's middle, so that the whole backdrop stands within the headset's 50 m far plane, 2 m short of it, from anywhere on it, as from the yard; which is why the foothills' last ring is at 36 m. That is also inside the foothills' first ring, so it lies on the level meadow.
- **clear of the river's banks and the pool's rim** (`backdrop::BANKS`, `RIM`), on the higher ground beside the river's channel, above its water, so that it never climbs down into it: the river within 7 m of its middle for 12 m and more, the waterfall, the pool and the rock seen from the lookout.
- **clear of the fence**, its inner edge at least 0.9 m from it, of the pines and of the bush, and out of the views the backdrop keeps open.

## Walking it

Where participants walk on it is derived from what it draws, so that they stand, and the ring where a teleport lands is drawn, on it rather than beneath it: `model()` draws its track from crest to crest and its clearings, `clearings()`, one at each station and the bay at the lookout, as ground (`Shapes::ground`), `ground()` gives those triangles, and `crate::walkable` derives the walkable ground from them and the yard's earth, less what stands on them, its posts and plaques, the seat, the rock and the plants beside it among them (`solids()`, `beside()`). Only the way out, `GATEWAY`, is declared by hand, `way_out()`, two rectangles, from the yard's earth between the gate's posts to the fence at `EARTH`, and on to the loop at `HEIGHT`, since the gate's posts stand within the clearance kept from what stands on the ground. The track at `HEIGHT` and the clearings at `CLEARING_HEIGHT` are within the centimetre a backstep goes up or down of the yard's earth, so that nothing stops at the gate; keep them so if the track is raised or lowered. Draw as ground only what participants should walk on: not the edges' outer slopes down to the meadow, nor anything that stands on the track. The client crate's `locomotion()` declares the derived ground and the way out. A pair of teleport pads, `pads()`, joins the yard, beside the way to the gate, and the lookout, in its bay, off the trail so that nobody walking or teleporting along it lands on one.

Two teleport targets, `targets()`, take whoever points at them there (see Tomori's `design/locomotion.md`): the waterfall rock in the pool (see `backdrop.md`), pointed at from the lookout or anywhere within 15 m, which takes them onto its flat top, facing the waterfall, where its sound is loudest; and the lookout's seat, pointed at from the rock, which takes them back onto the track before it, facing the pool. The rock's top is where they stand, not a surface, so that it is reached only by pointing at it, and a backstep there steps nowhere, off it into the water; the water and the banks within its spread are on no surface. The hens keep to `flock::AREA`, well inside the fence and short of the gate, startled or not. Two more, the way up from the rock to the upper terrace and back down, are the terrace's (see `terrace.md`).

## The stations and places

`STATIONS` are, in order from the gate: 10 squats; 10 shoulder rolls and 10 neck tilts, 5 each side; 20 calf raises; 10 lunges each side; and 30 arm circles and 3 deep breaths, 15 circles each way. Each is a count, never a time, which nobody can keep easily while doing it. None is on the floor or leans on anything, since the posts and rails are not there to lean on. Each has a clearing joined to the trail, on the side with room within `REACH`, its post at the clearing's edge facing it, and its plaque, `plaques()`, which the client crate draws in text from the renderer's glyph atlas.

`places()` are the named anchors Tomori's own server declares (`tomori_server_app::farmyard_trail`), each with room, `ROOM`, for one to eighteen workers: `riverbank`, `pool-lookout`, `waterfall-rock` and `upper-terrace`, kind `viewpoint`, the rock's beside it towards the lookout, behind whoever stands on it facing the fall, 1.8 m above its top, facing it, within 20 m of where participants arrive, so that the agent can come along, and the terrace's above it likewise behind whoever arrives there (see `terrace.md`); and `station-1` to `station-5`, kind `station`, labelled with their exercises, so that the agent can suggest a walk, go to a station and say what to do. A station's anchor stands beside its clearing, opposite its post, a little above head height, facing the clearing, and tells the agent of whoever comes within `STATION_RADIUS`, 1.8 m across the ground (`notify_within`), its clearing and the trail beside it, but never where participants arrive, the gate or a pad, so that arriving, leaving the yard or stepping off a pad tells it nothing. Beside the lookout's seat stands the campsite (see `campsite.md`). `solids()`, the posts, the seat and the rock, and `beside()`, the backdrop's plants and pines and the tree ferns' trunks and crowns (see `ferns.md`) within `BESIDE`, 5 m, of the trail or the rock, join the yard's, so that the agent, walls and panels keep clear of them: the agent comes 2 m from whoever it follows, which on a 1.2 m trail is often off it, and swings round them rather than standing in a tree fern or a pine.

## Drawing it

`trail::model()` is one model: the track, `LIFT` above the yard's earth, 8 mm, and so 18 mm above the meadow, with edges `EDGE` higher, open where the way out or a clearing meets it; the clearings, 6 mm below the track; a few stones along its edges; the posts and their boards; and the seat. It is counted in the backdrop's budget (see `backdrop.md`).

## Changing it

Move it through `WAYPOINTS`, and the stations by the point of the loop each stands at. Run the farmyard's tests: they check that it stays within reach, outside the fence and clear of the banks, the bush and the views, that it is walkable end to end, through the gate, without a gap or a step that stops a backstep, that landing anywhere on it lands within a millimetre of the top of what is drawn there, that the stations are spaced, beside it and labelled, their radii clear of the arrival, the gate, the pads and where the targets take participants, that the pads are off the way, that the targets take them onto the rock facing the fall and back to the trail facing the pool, that the water is on no surface and the rock's top reached only by pointing at it, and that the hens stay in. Set `TOMORI_FARMYARD_TRAIL_PREVIEW` to a PNG path when running the client crate's tests to save the trail by the river, the gate, the lookout and a plaque, as `-1.png` to `-4.png`. A change to the trail's anchors, or to what an earlier client would not draw, raises the farmyard's version.

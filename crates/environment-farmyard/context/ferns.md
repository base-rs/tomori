# The Farmyard's Ferns

Ferns grow close about the trail, near enough to be seen and walked among (`ferns.rs`). Tree ferns stand along the bush's edge on the left, on the riverbank, about the waterfall's pool and the campsite, and a few by the fence: ponga, the silver fern, its fronds silver beneath; mamaku, the black tree fern, tallest, its trunk and stalks black, by the water; and wheki, short and rough under a long skirt of dead fronds, where there is little room, as by the fence. Ground ferns grow in drifts along the trail's edges, on the river's banks and round the campsite: crown fern, a shuttlecock of stiff fronds; kiokio, arching, its young fronds flushed red; and hen-and-chickens fern, soft and drooping, in the shade. Koru, young fronds still curled, unfurl at the heart of every other tree fern and a few ground ferns. The bush's own tree ferns, which the backdrop places by the water (see `backdrop.md`), are drawn the same way, as ponga or mamaku, as large as before (`ferns::bush`).

## Ferns, not palms

A frond is a long strip, widest a third of the way along its blade, on a bare stalk, cut into segments, each with a leaflet either side, alternately, swept towards its tip and apart from the next, so that its edges are notched and the sky shows between them: finely divided in low poly. A tree fern's crown is 14 to 18 long fronds, held out flat as an umbrella, wider than it is deep, the older third lower and drooping, its tips below its heart, rather than a palm's few fronds arching up and over; its slender trunk flares at its foot, its faces alternately lighter and darker as fibres, and ponga and wheki have skirts of brown, dead fronds. Each point of a mesh carries its underside's colour, which the shader shows where a frond's back is seen: ponga's silver, every other a little paler.

## Swaying

Each point carries how far it sways at most, its frond tips most, 14 cm on a tree fern and 6 cm on a ground fern, scaled by the fern, its trunk and heart not at all. The client's `fern.wgsl` moves it by the time alone: a slow swell and a quicker stir, at each fern's own phase, leaning a little with the breeze, and a flutter of each frond by where it is on its fern. Nothing is uploaded after the scenery is made but the time, and the instances when the detail changes.

## Where they stand

`ferns::ferns` places them deterministically, from `props::scatter`, so every client draws the same ferns: tree ferns, then ground ferns, tried beside the trail, about the campsite, along the river's banks and, a tree fern, the pool's, and a ground fern, a third of the time, beside one already placed, in drifts; kept where it is shady enough (`shady`, the open front beside the gate least, with no tree ferns there but at its corners); of the kind that grows there, or the next that fits, each kind up to its most (`KINDS`). `fits` keeps each:

- **off everywhere participants stand or walk** (`from_standing`): the trail's band and its edges, the way out, its clearings, the campsite's clearing, the beach's shingle and the rock's top, and beyond the fence; a ground fern's fronds, a tree fern's trunk, and its crown too, 0.4 m back, unless it hangs `OVERHEAD`, 2.2 m above, where one walks beneath it; a ground fern within 5 m of where they do, to be seen;
- out of the water, a ground fern on the banks' tops and a tree fern just back from them, off the waterfall's cliff and the knoll behind it, and away from the notice board;
- apart from the backdrop's plants, the pines, the river's stones and the trail's and campsite's solids, and from the ferns placed before it, on ground it can stand on;
- out of the views the backdrop keeps open, and the trail's viewpoints' sights of the river, the waterfall and the pool (`sights`);
- a tree fern out of the anchors' rooms, one to eighteen workers fitting at each as the server lays them out (`clusters`), 0.4 m from each anchor's point or a metre below it, and leaving the agent room to come alongside anyone on the trail from 20 of 36 ways round them, or as many as before (`taken`).

A tree fern is two solids (`Fern::solids`), its trunk and its crown with its skirt, which `trail::beside` adds to the server's farmyard solids, so that the agent, walls and panels keep clear of them; a ground fern is decoration, off every walkable surface, below knee to waist high, and needs none. Where participants walk is derived from what the yard and the trail draw as ground less what stands on it (`walkable`), and no fern stands on that ground nor, solid or not, cuts any of it: a tree fern's trunk stands beyond its clearance, and its crown reaches over it only high enough overhead to leave its room clear.

## The Quest budget

Each kind is a mesh, drawn near, within `NEAR`, 10 m of the eyes, or far, with two thirds of its fronds or fewer and fewer segments, about half the triangles or less, and a koru not at all; the scenery groups the instances by mesh each frame the details change, and draws each mesh with all its instances in one draw, all with one pipeline, at most thirteen draws: ponga 491 and 214 triangles, mamaku 442 and 193, wheki 371 and 147, crown fern 99 and 35, kiokio and hen-and-chickens 90 and 28, and a koru 18. There are 152 near the trail, 22 tree ferns, 100 ground ferns and 30 koru, and the bush's 40. From anywhere participants stand they draw at most 22,151 triangles, under `ferns::BUDGET`, 24,000, a budget of their own, since they are near foliage the backdrop's does not count, and about 17,000 from the arrival; all of them near would be 38,000. With the backdrop's 17,110, which no longer counts its tree ferns' 4,360, the scenery beyond the yard is at most about 39,000 triangles, a little over the 22,000 the backdrop drew alone, which the Quest 3 bears easily; its shader is the models' lighting and a few sines a vertex.

## Changing it

Shape a kind in `shape`, and where they grow through `candidate`, `shady`, `kinds` and `KINDS`. Run the farmyard's tests: they check that the ferns are the same every time, of every kind, with koru at their hearts; that each stands on the ground, out of the water, off everywhere anyone stands or walks, beyond the fence, its crown over a walkable place only high overhead; that they keep the views, the status wall, the agent's home and its wall, the anchors' rooms and the agent's way to anyone on the trail open; that every rule refuses a fern somewhere; that each mesh is flat-faced, within its extent, a tree fern's crown wide and drooping, its tips swaying most and its trunk not at all, ponga silver beneath; and the triangle counts, which should be updated here too. The client crate's tests check that a ponga sways, is silver from beneath and green from above, and is not drawn before the reveal reaches it, and that every fern is drawn once in its detail; set `TOMORI_FARMYARD_FERNS_PREVIEW` to a PNG path to save the trail by the river and the campsite, as `-1.png` and `-2.png`.

# The Farmyard's Campsite

Beside the lookout's log seat, a step west of its end, between the trail and the tree ferns on the pool's bank, a campsite stands on a clearing of packed earth: a ring of stones round a fire pit, a bonfire of leaning logs, a second log seat across the fire, and four marshmallow sticks leaning over that seat towards the fire. It is all in `campsite.rs`, laid out from `campsite::FIRE`, and drawn by the client crate.

## Where it is

`FIRE` is the pit's middle; `seat()` the second seat, as `trail::seat()` gives the lookout's; `standing()` four discs at the trail's height on the fire's trail and lookout sides, which the client crate declares walkable, joined to the trail; `solids()` the pit with its flames and the second seat with its sticks; and `place()` the `campsite` anchor, kind `area`, high above the fire, facing the trail, with `ROOM` for one to eighteen workers, which `tomori_server_app::farmyard_campsite` declares. Where participants stand is within `REACH`, 12.6 m, of the backdrop's middle, so that the backdrop stays within the far plane a metre short of it. `clearing()` is the edge of its earth, drawn just above the ground where it rises towards the pool.

## The sticks

The sticks are loose bodies of `tomori-physics`, as the beach's stones are, numbered from `FIRST_STICK` (`body`, `index`). `hull()` is a stick's rod and marshmallow, six-sided (`STICK_SIDES`), about its middle, its butt towards +Z; `bodies()` declares each with `Body::convex`, of dry wood (`DENSITY`, lighter than water), leaning on its log at `home(index)`; `colliders()`, `waters()` and `settings()` are the campsite's world, its ground, logs, ring of stones, bonfire and pool, within `REGION` of the fire. `Pose` is where a stick is, its butt and the way along it to its tip: `Pose::of(placement)` reads it from its body, `Pose::racked(index)` leans it on its log, touching it, and `Pose::held(hand, turn)` runs it forward through the fist, as `grip()` holds its body, `HOLD` from its butt; `drawn(placement)` places its model. `heat(point)` is how hot it is for a marshmallow, `toasted` how far it toasts there in a while, at `TOAST_RATE` at full heat, through `GOLDEN`, `BROWN` and `BLACK`, drawn in `SHADES`, `shade` and `colour`; black in the heat, it is `burning`. `by_its_log`, `goes_back` and `in_pool` say where a stick let go or at rest stays and where it goes back on its log.

What every client is told: `Shared`, the sticks' bodies and how toasted each is, in the farmyard's state as `campsite` (`shared`), and `Event`, what changed of their bodies and their toasting. Participants take and let go of them by `tomori-physics`'s own intents, as stones. The server side's part is `side::Sticks`, on `side::Loose`, which the beach's `Shingle` shares: it takes and lets go as any loose body, toasts what is held from where the server saw the hand, puts back what is let go gently by its log or comes to rest where it does not stay, freshens the marshmallow of a stick as it begins its way back to its log, which clients fade out as it was, and tidies an empty campsite after `TIDY`, an hour. The client crate's `campsite.rs` is its client, on its `loose.rs`.

## Drawing it

`model()` is the clearing, stones, ash, logs and second seat, lit, and the embers, glowing; `stick()` a stick with a marshmallow in each shade, of which an instance shows one. The client crate draws the flames, embers and burning marshmallows' flames with its `fire.wgsl`, and lights the campsite with a flickering lamp. `BUDGET` bounds their triangles together, which the tests count.

## Changing it

Move it through `FIRE`; the seat, sticks, standing and anchor follow. Run the farmyard's tests: they check that it stays beside the lookout's seat, off the trail, clear of the pool, the river, the bush, the pines, the yard and the views, within its reach, its clearing above the ground, the sticks touching their log and out of the heat, the sticks' hull, where they rest and go back, the toasting, and the budget; the server side's tests, the sticks taken, toasted, let go, put back, floating and tidied; and the server program's, that one to eighteen workers fit its anchor clear of its solids. Set `TOMORI_FARMYARD_CAMPSITE_PREVIEW` to a PNG path when running the client crate's tests to save the campsite from the lookout's seat. A change to its anchor, or to what an earlier client would not draw, raises the farmyard's version.
